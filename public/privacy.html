Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

At LetterPro, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and password when you create an account
  • Request Information: Letter type, description, recipient email, and any files you upload
  • Payment Information: Payment receipts you submit (we do not store credit card or bank details)

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information (browser type, operating system)
  • Usage data (pages visited, time spent on site)
  • IP address

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve our letter writing services
  • Process your requests and deliver completed letters
  • Send you order updates and status notifications
  • Communicate with you about your requests
  • Process payments and verify transactions
  • Prevent fraud and ensure service security

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information:

  • Service Providers: With trusted third parties who assist in operating our service (email delivery, payment processing)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or governmental authority
  • With Your Consent: In any other circumstances with your explicit permission

4. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS)
  • Secure data storage
  • Access controls and authentication requirements
  • Regular security assessments

6. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies to:

  • Keep you logged in to your account
  • Remember your preferences
  • Understand how you use our website

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Raise concerns about how we handle your data

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting a notice on our website or sending an email.
